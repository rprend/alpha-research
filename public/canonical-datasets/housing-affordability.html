Dataset Briefing

This public housing affordability proof dataset combines HUD Fair Market Rent benchmarks with Census/ACS income and HUD CHAS rent-burden context. It is shaped for ranking current demo-county pressure from FY2024 rent benchmarks and ACS/CHAS burden context while keeping source caveats attached to the answer.
