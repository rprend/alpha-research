Daily packets — the curriculum as 133 days

The whole curriculum, cut into 133 daily packets of 45–90 minutes, exactly one link each. Total: ~170 hours. Five days a week ≈ 6 months; every day ≈ 4.5 months.

How a day works

  1. Open today's packet in the phase file. It has four parts: a time box, one link, Do (the exact instruction — which minutes of which video, which sections, which build step), Explains (the one concept you're buying today), Done when (a binary check).
  2. Do the Do. When the Done when is true — and only then — tick the box below. That checkbox list is your entire progress-tracking system.
  3. Done early? Stop anyway. Over the time box but not done? Stop at ~90 minutes and finish tomorrow before ticking — don't renumber anything, the numbers are just sequence, not calendar dates.

Rules

  • Never read ahead of your day — that's the whole method (README). Re-reading behind you is always allowed.
  • Missed days cost nothing. No streaks. Resume at the unticked box.
  • "Done when" is the gate, not time spent. If you can't pass a checkpoint day cold, redo the days it names — that's the system working, not failing.
  • Money days are marked 💲 below; the Do line states the expected cost before you launch anything. Total GPU spend across all 133 days: ~$100–200 if you follow the packets.
  • You are finished when Day 133 is ticked and capstone-plan.md is committed — at that point you exit into Phase 6's research practice with a chosen project.
