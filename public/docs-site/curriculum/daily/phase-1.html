Phase 1 daily packets — The Transformer (Days 15–35)

Builds a GPT from an empty file — BPE tokenizer to sampled fake Shakespeare — then reads the 2017–2019 canon as documentation for your own code. Prerequisite: Phase 0 checkpoint passed (backprop from scratch, fluent shape-reading).

Day 15 — BPE tokenizer video, part 1

⏱ ~80 min · Link: Let's build the GPT Tokenizer

Do: Watch 0:00–1:10 at 1.5x, typing every line into a fresh tokenizer/ dir in your curriculum repo: UTF-8 bytes, pair counting, the merge loop, the vocab (background: section 1.1 of the phase guide).

Explains: A tokenizer is two artifacts on disk — a string→int table and a merge list — and BPE is just "repeatedly mint a token for the most frequent adjacent pair."

Done when: Your typed code trains merges on the video's sample text and your printed merge list matches his on-screen output.

Day 16 — BPE tokenizer video, part 2

⏱ ~80 min · Link: Let's build the GPT Tokenizer

Do: Watch 1:10–end at 1.5x, still typing: encode/decode, the GPT-2/GPT-4 regex split patterns, special tokens, and the tokenization-weirdness tour (counting letters, arithmetic, SolidGoldMagikarp).

Explains: Why models can't count the r's in "strawberry" — the model literally never sees letters, only chunk ids, and degenerate chunks cause degenerate behavior.

Done when: Your encode/decode pass decode(encode(s)) == s on a paragraph containing emoji and non-ASCII text.

Day 19 — Attention on paper, shapes everywhere

Day 24 — Sampling loop by hand

⏱ ~60 min · Link: Phase 1 guide

Day 29 — Attention Is All You Need, part 2
