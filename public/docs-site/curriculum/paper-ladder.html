The Paper Ladder — a graded reader for AI research

Papers in the order they become *readable*, Lingua Latina style: each entry assumes only the vocabulary of its phase. Bold = core canon (read fully at its phase). Others = skim or reference. arXiv IDs given where they exist; Anthropic interp work lives at transformer-circuits.pub; some foundational work is blog-only (noted — this field's canon is part blog posts, which is itself worth knowing).

Format: [phase] Title (year, ID) — what it gives you.

Readable after Phase 0 (foundations)

  • [0] A Neural Probabilistic Language Model (Bengio 2003) — your makemore MLP, as a paper; calibrates how far the field has come.
  • [0] The Unreasonable Effectiveness of RNNs (Karpathy 2015, blog) — pre-transformer context, pleasure read.
  • [0] Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization (2014, 1412.6980) — skim §2; you use it daily.
  • [0] *Prehistory, optional:* Sequence to Sequence Learning (2014, 1409.3215); Bahdanau attention (2014, 1409.0473) — where "attention" was invented, as a translation alignment trick.

Readable after Phase 1 (the transformer)

  • [1] Attention Is All You Need (2017, 1706.03762) — the founding document; remember: encoder–decoder, your GPT is the decoder half.
  • [1] GPT-1: Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training (2018, OpenAI) — the pretrain-then-adapt thesis.
  • [1] GPT-2: Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners (2019, OpenAI) — scale → zero-shot prompting.
  • [1] BERT (2018, 1810.04805) — the encoder-only branch (masked LM); historically huge, evolutionary dead end for generation; read to know why it lost.
  • [1] RoFormer / RoPE (2021, 2104.09864) — §3 only: how rotary position embedding works.
  • [1] GQA (2023, 2305.13245) — one figure's worth.
  • [1] T5 (2019, 1910.10683) — skim; the "everything is text-to-text" framing + a model family evals still use.

Readable after Phase 2 (scale)

  • [2] GPT-3: Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (2020, 2005.14165) — in-context learning; the prompting era begins.
  • [2] The Llama 3 Herd of Models (2024, 2407.21783) — the most complete public picture of a modern full-stack training effort; will be re-read in Phase 3.
  • [2] FineWeb (2024, 2406.17557 + HF blog) — what data work is.
  • [2] Megatron-LM (2019, 1909.08053) — tensor parallelism, figures only.
  • [2] OPT (2022, 2205.01068) + its public logbook — the sociology of a big run.
  • [2] PaLM (2022, 2204.02311) — skim; emergent-abilities discourse source. Pair with: Emergent Abilities (2206.07682) vs. Mirage of Emergent Abilities (2304.15004) — a complete, instructive scientific argument about measurement.
  • [2] Tensor Programs V / muP (2022, 2203.03466) — abstract + figures; hyperparameter transfer exists.
  • [2] FlashAttention (2022, 2205.14135) — readable now, canonical in Phase 4.

How to use this list

Never read ahead of your phase out of ambition — that's reading Cicero in week 3, and it produces the illusion of understanding that this curriculum exists to prevent. Read *behind* your phase freely: re-reading GPT-3 after Phase 5 is a different, better paper. Total core canon (bold): 24 papers. A working researcher's actual edge is not having read more papers than this — it's having *built* what the papers describe, which by this point you have.
