    Tate Britain

    • Tate Britain : Exhibition Barbara Hepworth: Sculpture for a Modern World

      24 June – 25 October 2015

      This major retrospective charts Hepworth’s progress from her earliest surviving carvings to the large-scale bronzes of the 1960s. Among the highlights are four large sculptures in sumptuous African hardwood – the high point of her post-war carving career – reunited in one room

      Book now

    • Tate Britain : Exhibition Fighting History

      9 June – 13 September 2015

      Focusing on the conflict, martyrdom and catastrophe found in history painting, this exhibition celebrates the emotional power of history painting and show its persistent place in art from the eighteenth century to the present day

      Book now

    Tate Modern

    • Tate Modern : Exhibition The EY Exhibition: Sonia Delaunay

      15 April – 9 August 2015

      This exhibition is the first UK retrospective to assess the breadth of her vibrant artistic practice, featuring her groundbreaking paintings, textiles and clothes she made across a sixty-year career, as well as the results of her innovative collaborations from Diaghilev to Liberty

      Book now

    • Tate Modern : Exhibition Agnes Martin

      3 June – 11 October 2015

      Join us for the first retrospective of Martin’s work since 1994. Covering the full breadth of her practice, this exhibition reveals Martin’s little known experiments and traces her development from biomorphic abstraction to her mesmerising grid and striped canvases

      Book now

    Tate Liverpool

    • Tate Liverpool : Exhibition Jackson Pollock: Blind Spots

      30 June – 18 October 2015

      This exhibition takes vistors on a journey through Pollock’s artist practice, exploring his immensely rich and relatively neglected body of work and will provide new insights into this pivotal artist’s contribution to and influence on post-war art

      Book now

    • Tate Liverpool : Exhibition Glenn Ligon: Encounters and Collisions

      30 June – 18 October 2015

      Glenn Ligon who is deeply involved in the legacy of Post-War American art, highlights the cultural and social histories of the time, such as the civil rights movement. The exhibition features many major figures such as Willem de Kooning, Jackson Pollock and Chris Ofili

      Book now

    • Tate Liverpool : Exhibition Geta Bratescu

      30 June – 18 October 2015

      This introduction to Bratescu’s singular vision comprises a selection of works from 1960 to the present day which showcases her commitment to the drawn line, whether created in fabric with a sewing machine, by the body performing through space, or found within her detailed classical draughtsmanship

      Read more

    • Tate Liverpool : Exhibitions and displays DLA Piper Series: Constellations

      Open every day

      Take a fresh look at the connection between artworks in Tate collection with this ongoing exhibit which challenges formal modes of display

      Read more

    • Tate Liverpool : Courses and workshops Five Week Course: Jackson Pollock

      Mondays 28 September – 26 October 2015, 10.30–12.30

      Join Ed Bruce, Artist and University of Liverpool Continuing Education lecturer, who will lead a five-week course that explores Jackson Pollock’s heroic and mythical status as a leading figure of post-war American art

      Book now
