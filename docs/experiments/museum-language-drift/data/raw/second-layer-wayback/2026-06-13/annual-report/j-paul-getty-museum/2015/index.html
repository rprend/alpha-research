Financial Information

As a nonprofit private foundation, The J. Paul Getty Trust is an exempt organization under section 501 (c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. As a private operating foundation, the Getty must file a Form 990-PF with the IRS each year. This annual reporting return provides information on The Trust's mission, programs, and finances, including the fair market value of all assets, an analysis of revenue and expenses, and a complete listing of grants made.

The Getty's complete Form 990-PF and Form 990-T for the past three (3) fiscal years are available on this page, as is information about senior staff compensation, The Trust's debt transactions, and fiscal year 2010 audited financial statements.
