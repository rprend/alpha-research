# Cultural Observatory Research Backlog

This folder tracks candidate Archive Signals / Cultural Observatory studies before they become full experiment folders.

The backlog exists to keep AI-assisted research scouting honest. A proposal is useful only when it names a measurable unit of analysis, plausible sources, expected transformations, failure modes, and a publication angle.

## Files

- `backlog.json`: machine-readable study queue.
- `scout-template.md`: prompt and audit template for AI research-scout runs.

## Status Values

- `idea`: editorially interesting but not yet source-probed.
- `source-probed`: at least one source has been tested and a manifest or access note exists.
- `corpus-built`: raw and processed data exist.
- `analysis-ready`: processed tables and report artifacts exist.
- `drafted`: public draft exists.
- `publishable`: readiness gate passes and claims are source-backed.
- `blocked`: source access, rights, quality, or scope makes the study impractical for now.

## Acceptance Standard

A backlog item should not become an experiment folder until it has:

- one clear research question
- one primary unit of analysis
- at least two plausible sources or one complete source
- a likely public essay angle
- known quality risks
- the first runnable corpus build

The goal is not to collect ideas. The goal is to turn a small number of strong ideas into repeatable, source-backed public essays.
