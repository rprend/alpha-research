Welcome

Our university’s purpose is found in our teaching, scholarship, and research, which contribute knowledge, understanding, and breakthroughs to society and affirm the tangible connection between our efforts and the everyday lives of people across the nation and the world.

This purpose is expressed most fundamentally by Yale’s core mission of creating, disseminating, and preserving knowledge. Our core mission has enduringly guided our university, and it will continue to do so. In pursuing this mission, Yale’s faculty and students have addressed global challenges, generational opportunities, and perpetual questions about the human condition, aspiring to solve the seemingly unsolvable and answer the seemingly unanswerable.
