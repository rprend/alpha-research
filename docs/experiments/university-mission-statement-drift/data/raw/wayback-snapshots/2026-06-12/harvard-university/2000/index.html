General information

Introduction to Harvard
The Virtual Tour of Harvard presents an online walking tour of Harvard Yard. The comprehensive Harvard Guide contains general facts, history, Harvard lore, and sightseeing information. A College in a Yard is a multimedia tour of Harvard College, the undergraduate program at the University (requires RealAudio).

FAQ
Our "Frequently-Asked Questions about Harvard University" section answers more than 40 of the most common inquiries that we receive here in the Webmaster's office. Lots of good information on a wide range of topics!

Telephone and Email Directory
The centrally-maintained Staff and Student directory provides information about students, faculty, and staff throughout the University. If you can't find what you need online, you can contact one of the telephone information numbers for more help.

What is Harvard's mailing address?
Due to the size of the Harvard campus, there is no single mailing address that will work throughout the school. We've compiled a list of the most frequently-requested addresses.

Guide to Harvard acronyms
Mystified by the alphabet soup of Harvard jargon? (FAS? HIO? GSE?) To help visitors unfamiliar with our abbreviations, we've compiled a list of Harvard acronyms.

Harvard Statistics
"Quick Facts" is a good overview of Harvard's size and scope. (Harvard News Office). The Harvard Fact Book, published annually by the Office of Budget and Financial Planning, contains extensive information about the University as an institution: enrollments, degrees conferred, administrative structure, and resources.

Harvard gift items

Harvard Collections
The Harvard Collections store in Holyoke Center features a unique assortment of gift ideas inspired by the museums at Harvard. Proceeds from each sale help support the University's museums and collections

Harvard Cooperative Society
Affectionately known as The Coop (rhymes with "loop"), this campus bookstore carries Harvard t-shirts and other insignia merchandise in their online catalog.

Harvard Museum of Natural History gift shop
Totebags, t-shirts, publications, slides, and other gift items drawn from the Museum's collections.

Harvard Student Agencies
The HSA sells Harvard t-shirts, banners, and other insignia merchandise through their campus store and online catalog.

Harvard University Art Museums Shop
An online catalog of Harvard Art Museum publications.

Houghton Library Shop
A selection of cards and posters from the Houghton Library Department of Printing and Graphic Arts. Images are taken from manuscripts, rare books, prints, and photos held by this prestigious collection.
