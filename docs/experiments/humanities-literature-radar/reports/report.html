Article Argument

This first field map is evidence that the project should be framed as a cultural-observatory publication rather than as a narrow technical demo. The corpus can support essays about field formation, method diffusion, and the movement of topics across disciplines, but publishable trend claims need larger backfills and source-coverage audits.

Next Essays

  1. The Rise Of AI And The Humanities: The corpus already tracks AI, LLM, computational, and digital-humanities records across scholarly sources.
  2. Canon Drift: Canon, reception, citation, syllabi, and translation records can seed a broader corpus about cultural memory.
  3. What Changed In University Mission Statements?: This should be run as a separate longitudinal web corpus because it needs institution pages and historical snapshots, not only publication metadata.

Caveats

  • This first artifact uses public metadata and abstracts where available, not full-text scholarship.
  • Open indexes underrepresent books, chapters, non-English work, and paywalled humanities journals.
  • Trend claims require larger backfills and source-coverage checks before publication.
