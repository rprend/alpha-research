Center for the Study of the Novel | Department of English Skip to main content Stanford University Stanford Department of English School of Humanities and Sciences Search Main content start Center for the Study of the Novel The Center for the Study of the Novel promotes conversation on the novel and related narrative genres as these forms have been practiced across history and cultures. CSN is committed to the importance of studying literature as a primary form of human expression, even as it examines what interdisciplinary perspectives may tell us about literature and the novel in particular. CSN further is committed to studying the history and practice of literary criticism and theory illuminating the novel and its relations to society and culture.
