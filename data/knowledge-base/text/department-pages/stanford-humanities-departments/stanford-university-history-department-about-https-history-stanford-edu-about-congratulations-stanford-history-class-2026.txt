Stanford's Department of History will host its 2026 commencement ceremony at Lane History Corner in Citrus Courtyard on Sunday, June 14, 2026 from 12:30pm - 2:30pm (PDT). Citrus Courtyard is behind History Corner (Bldg. 200) at 450 Jane Stanford Way, Stanford, CA 94305 . Please see the map below, showing walking instructions from the University Commencement Ceremony (Stanford Stadium) to Citrus Courtyard. We in Stanford Department of History look forward to celebrating our graduates’ accomplishments. While we firmly support freedom of speech, disturbances to the ceremony will not be tolerated. If you would like to express your opinion on an issue, please do so in White Plaza, Stanford's free speech zone ( directions & info ). Thank you for respecting this celebratory moment for our graduates and their guests. Awards will be announced at the ceremony.
