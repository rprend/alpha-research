New courses included Literature Beyond the Book: Small Magazines in Africa and the Diaspora , Poetry and Photography , and Shakespeare and Film . We welcomed Blackburn Artist in Residence Frances Leviston (who will be staying here through the fall), and we anticipate the arrival of several new professors next year.
