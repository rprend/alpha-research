Welcome | Department of Comparative Literature Skip to main content Department of Comparative Literature Menu Yale Faculty of Arts and Sciences Search Department of Comparative Literature Yale Faculty of Arts and Sciences Search Welcome to the Comparative Literature Department For over 50 years, Yale’s Comparative Literature department has been one of the preeminent sites, worldwide, for the comparative practice of literary history and analysis, and for the promulgation of literary theory. From its founding as a unique program for wide-ranging, cross-cultural, philologically and theoretically engaged studies of language and literature, the department has been committed to a broad geographic and intellectual scope, both in its graduate curriculum as well as its vibrant undergraduate course of study. Located at the heart of Yale’s campus, we are a center for multidisciplinary scholarship in over twenty languages, connecting our students and faculty to variety of departments, institutes, and working groups within Yale and beyond. Connections on Campus In addition to our own strong faculty, Comparative Literature has close connections to other stellar departments at Yale that our students could work with , both through its joint programs and a variety of research projects and working groups.
