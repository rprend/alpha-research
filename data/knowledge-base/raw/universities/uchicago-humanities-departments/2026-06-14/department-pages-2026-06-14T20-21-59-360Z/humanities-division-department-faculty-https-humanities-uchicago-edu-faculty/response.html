Frequently Asked Questions

  • Who should I contact if I have questions about my computer?
  • Where can I find a list of recent faculty honors, creative endeavors, and publications?
  • Where can I find information about a specific faculty member?

The activities of Divisional faculty members drive the world-renowned research and pedagogic work of the University of Chicago. The results of these efforts are published books, influential conferences, new works of art, important research discoveries, and graduates whose critical thinking and scholarship has given UChicago its reputation for passionate teaching as well as innovative thinking. An infrastructure of appointment processes, financial policies and procedures, personnel practices, and technology makes these achievements possible. The links here lead to information and resources both for and about our faculty.
