Alex Levine Publishes Article in Geschichte und Gesellschaft

April 16, 2026/Student Highlights by Frank Masur

Graduate student Alex Levine just had his first article published in the history journal Geschichte und Gesellschaft (History and Society).

The article titled “Geneva’s Environment and the League of Nations” is an urban environmental study of international organizations in Geneva between the two world wars, giving an understanding of how the city and region as a lived space impacted institutions we normally mythologize.

Read the full article: https://www.vr-elibrary.de/doi/10.13109/gege.2025.51.1.70
