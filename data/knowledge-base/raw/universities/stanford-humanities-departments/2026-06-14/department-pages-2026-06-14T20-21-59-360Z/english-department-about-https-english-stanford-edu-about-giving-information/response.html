Giving Information

Teaching and research in English is an essential part of a liberal arts education. We are dedicated to teaching our students to think critically and creatively, to write effectively and imaginatively, and to develop a deep understanding of the human experience, past and present.

A gift to the Stanford English department can support many aspects of our educational mission, such as grants and prizes for undergraduate research and fellowship support for our graduate program.
