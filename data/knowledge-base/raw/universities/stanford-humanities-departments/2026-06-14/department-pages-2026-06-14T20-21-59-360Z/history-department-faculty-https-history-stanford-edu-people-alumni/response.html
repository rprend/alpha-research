Alumni

Our students have gone on to pursue careers across a wide range of fields, from politics to professional sports.  This page features some of their  post-graduation paths and memories of the History Department. If you are one of our alumni and would like to receive periodic updates about our activities, please contact  our External Relations Officer Vivian Beebe Sana at vbeebe at stanford dot edu.
