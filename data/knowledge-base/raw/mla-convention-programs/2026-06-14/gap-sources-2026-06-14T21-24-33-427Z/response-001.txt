        <h1 id="pagetitle">2027 MLA Convention</h1>
                    <div class="fieldtype-xmltext">
                <div class="ezrichtext-field"><p>The 2027&nbsp;MLA Annual Convention will be held from 7&nbsp;to 10&nbsp;January in Los Angeles.&nbsp;The&nbsp;presidential theme will be <a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Presidential-Theme-for-the-2027-Convention">Emancipatory Narratives</a>. We look forward to meeting in person, and we hope you&rsquo;ll join us!</p><p><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/A-Letter-from-MLA-Executive-Director-Paula-M.-Krebs-Urging-Support-of-Convention-Attendance" class=" file--krebs-urging-support-of-convention-attendance">A Letter from Executive Director Paula M. Krebs Urging Support of Convention Attendance&nbsp;</a></p><h2>Session Information</h2><p>Sessions at the 2027 MLA convention will take place either in person or online.&nbsp;Sessions are organized by members; nonmembers are welcome to attend.&nbsp;The deadline to submit most sessions for the 2027 MLA convention has passed. If you have an idea for a session on a late-breaking subject, consider <a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Proposing-a-Just-in-Time-Session">proposing a just-in-time session</a>.</p><ul><li><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Presidential-Theme-for-the-2027-Convention">2027 Presidential Theme: Emancipatory Narratives</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session">Planning a Convention Session</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session/Proposing-a-Special-Session">Proposing a Special Session</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session/Calls-for-Papers">2027 Calls for Papers</a></li><li><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Proposing-a-Just-in-Time-Session">Proposing a Just-in-Time Session</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session/Access-Guidelines-for-MLA-Convention-Session-Organizers-and-Presenters">Access Guidelines for MLA Convention Session Organizers and Presenters</a></li></ul><h2>General Information</h2><p>Registration for the 2027 MLA convention will open in September for MLA members and in October for nonmembers. Convention registration is required to participate in or attend meetings in-person or online and to take advantage of all the other convention experiences, including visiting the exhibit hall, using the MLA Professional Development Hub, and reserving hotel rooms at special MLA rates.</p><ul><li><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Registration-Rates-for-the-2027-Convention">2027 Registration Rates</a></li><li><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Registration-and-Hotels/2027-Registration-and-Housing-FAQs">Registration and Housing FAQs</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session/Convention-Session-FAQs">Convention Session FAQs</a></li><li><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/Online-Session-FAQ">Online Session FAQs</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session/Convention-Participant-FAQs">Convention Participant FAQs</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Code-of-Conduct">Appropriate Conduct at the MLA Annual Convention</a></li><li><a href="/Events/2027-MLA-Convention/MLA-Exhibit-Hall">Exhibit Hall</a></li><li><a href="/About-Us/Calendar/Meetings-and-Events">Convention&nbsp;Calendar</a></li><li><a href="/Events/Planning-a-Convention-Session/Future-Conventions">Future Conventions</a></li></ul><p>Are you interested in&nbsp;<a href="/Events/Exhibiting-and-Sponsorship">exhibiting or sponsorship</a>? Find out about the MLA exhibit hall and other opportunities for reaching MLA members.</p>
