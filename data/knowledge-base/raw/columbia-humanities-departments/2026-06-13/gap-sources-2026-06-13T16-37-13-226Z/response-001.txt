<p><span style="font-family: georgia, palatino, serif;color: #000000">Seth Low Professor History and Founding Director of the Center for Science and Society <strong>Pamela Smith</strong> has been awarded a three-year <a href="https://scienceandsociety.columbia.edu/news/founding-director-pamela-smith-awarded-getty-foundation-grant">Getty Foundation Grant</a>, with funds supporting research for the project &#8220;Minescapes: The Material Complex of Copper in Central Europe and Beyond.&#8221; The research will focus on the most important copper mines in the early modern world, which supplied Renaissance Europe with copper for bronze cannons and sculptures, and will bring together a team of historians, natural scientists, and art conservators.</span></p>
